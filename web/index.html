Most software asks you to type instructions over and over. MIMIR simply observes how you do things, learns your personal style, and steps in to help right when you need it.

What exactly is MIMIR?

MIMIR is a digital companion that lives on your computer. Instead of waiting for instructions, it watches how you work — the way you write, organize, fix mistakes, and finish tasks — and builds a quiet understanding of your personal style. Then it puts that understanding to work on new tasks, while you stay in charge.
