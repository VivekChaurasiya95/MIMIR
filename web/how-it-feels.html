How it feels

Three quiet moments, from first meeting to everyday ease.

  1. Quiet Observation

    It doesn't get in your way. While you write, edit, and organize, MIMIR quietly learns what you care about.

  2. Deep Understanding

    It doesn't just memorize clicks. It understands the principles behind your decisions — your habits, your taste, and your standards.

  3. Gentle Assistance

    When an unfamiliar task comes up, you don't have to write a giant prompt. MIMIR already knows how you'd do it.

Your first week together

No setup rituals, no configuration rabbit holes. Just this:

  1. Day one: it watches

    MIMIR sits quietly beside your work, noticing how you write, name, organize, and finish things.

  2. First week: it shares

    It shows you what it has noticed about your style — and you correct anything it got wrong.

  3. From then on: it helps

    New task? It drafts, tidies, and handles the familiar parts. You review, approve, and create.

A different approach

Why you shouldn't have to prompt everything.

Most software starts from zero every time you open it — the same questions, the same setup, the same explaining. MIMIR starts from you. It has watched how you work, so ordinary tasks arrive half done.

Before: type the same instructions, tune the same settings, repeat tomorrow.

With MIMIR: it remembers, suggests quietly, and only acts when you say so.
