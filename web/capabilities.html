What it can do

Six quiet superpowers, all learned from watching you — never configured.

Learns from your history

Every commit, draft, and revision is a lesson. MIMIR notices your commit style, your testing habits, and how you fix mistakes — the patterns you'd never think to write down.

Figures out your unwritten rules

Behind your habits sit standards: for example, always check tests before pushing, keep error messages specific, write small, focused commits. MIMIR distills these into lasting principles it can reuse anywhere.

Remembers what matters

Your standards live in a durable memory on your laptop. When a new task arrives, MIMIR recalls exactly the right ones — and keeps score of how well each rule holds up over time.

Writes the way you would

Drafts, code, and messages come out following your rules — your structure, your naming taste, your level of care. It can even show its work next to a generic draft, so the difference is visible.

Clicks and types for you

When words aren't enough, MIMIR has hands: a smooth-gliding cursor, natural typing, and terminal steps — and it freezes the instant your hand touches the mouse.

Talks it through

Ask in plain sentences, get plain answers — with a gentle on-screen presence that always shows what it's doing. See The Companion.

An open invitation

MIMIR is free and open source. Look inside, shape it, make it yours.
