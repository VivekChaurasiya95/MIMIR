Get Started

Your private space

An observing companion has to earn its place. These are non-negotiable.

Stays on your computer

Your personal habits and work stay private on your laptop. Everything MIMIR learns lives in a file you own — nothing is uploaded in order to learn.

Never records passwords

Smart filters strip out personal keys, passwords, and private notes before anything is remembered. The memory keeps rules, never secrets.

Instant pause, always

A single tap on the keyboard or mouse pauses MIMIR immediately — or just say the word. You are always in control.

Nothing hidden from you

Everything MIMIR believes about your style is open for you to read, correct, or delete at any time. No black boxes.

One honest footnote: spoken replies can use an optional cloud voice. What MIMIR learns about you never leaves your laptop either way.

An open invitation

MIMIR is free and open source. Look inside, shape it, make it yours.
